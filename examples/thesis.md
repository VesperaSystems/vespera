# Investment Thesis — Foundry Ventures (example)

We back B2B companies in the UK and EU with recurring software revenue at Series B,
writing £3–8m cheques. A deal fits our thesis when:

1. **Recurring revenue scale.** ARR above £5m, growing at least 40% year on year.
2. **Software economics.** Gross margin above 70%, with software an increasing share
   of revenue mix.
3. **Retention.** Net revenue retention above 110%.
4. **Clean IP.** The company must own all core intellectual property outright,
   including work produced by contractors.
5. **No structural supplier lock-in.** We avoid businesses bound by exclusive supply
   arrangements or take-or-pay purchase commitments that limit operating flexibility.
6. **Runway discipline.** At least 12 months of runway at close, before our capital.
7. **Governance.** Executed shareholder agreements and clean cap table documentation.

We pass on deep hardware plays with sub-50% blended margins and on any company where
core IP ownership is disputed or unresolved.
